# Nexo Plan Checklist

- Requirement sources and conflicts are explicit.
- Each relevant requirement maps to a testable acceptance criterion.
- Current architecture and dependency direction are described.
- Boundary, contracts, data, validation, failures, permissions, and NFRs are covered.
- Architecture/technology and `Pattern:` decisions are explicit.
- Compatibility, rollout, rollback, and observability are proportional.
- Maintenance delta names responsibility, owner, manual work, and abstraction trigger.
- Focused tests and evidence are named.
- No speculative dependency, service, configuration, or abstraction is introduced.
